Research

My research is focused on developing the mathematical and scientific foundations of modern AI. I've recently been interested in the following directions:

  • Representation Learning I study how neural networks learn useful representations through gradient-based training, and derive sharp, end-to-end learning guarantees in tractable model settings.
  • Transformer Capabilities I study when and how transformers can learn to perform different tasks -- such as in-context learning, reasoning, or factual recall -- during training, and characterize their capabilities and limitations.
  • Modern Optimization Theory I build optimization theory which captures how neural networks are trained in practice, clarifies the role of different optimization algorithms, and guides the design of new, practical algorithms.
